# STOPLINK: the company

Customer: truck-stop operators. Product: an airline-style miles rewards program that encourages repeat purchases. Drivers earn miles buying fuel and food, then redeem for real flights that STOPLINK books on existing airlines. Revenue comes from selling miles to operators. The spread must cover redemption and operating costs.

The orbital data center literally hosts the app and rewards database. The founders worried about AWS and regulators, so they massively overreacted by launching their own hosting infrastructure. There is no separate AI product or compute customer. Space is the absurd infrastructure choice; the loyalty program is the entire business.

Key lines: “It is literally just hosting the fucking app.” “AWS asked us to pick a region. Nadav selected away.” “Our lawyer said orbit does not fix the regulatory problem. We asked Will how much higher the rocket goes.”
